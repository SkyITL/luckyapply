Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bazi?
Bazi (八字), also known as Four Pillars of Destiny, is a Chinese astrological concept that analyzes a person's destiny based on their birth year, month, day, and hour. Each pillar contains a Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch representing different elemental energies.
How accurate are the calculations?
Our calculations are based on authentic classical Chinese metaphysics texts including Dripping Heavenly Marrow (滴天髓). We use traditional Bazi methodology that has been practiced for centuries. Results show when cosmic energies align favorably with your personal elements.
Do I need to know Chinese astrology to use the app?
No! Lucky Apply makes ancient wisdom accessible to everyone. Simply enter your birth details, select your date range, and receive clear, easy-to-understand recommendations.
What's included in the Premium subscription?
Premium unlocks unlimited lucky time calculations, lucky colors and directions for each time period, full reading history access, and priority customer support.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID account. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Lucky Apply, and tap Cancel Subscription. You must cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
Is my birth data private?
Yes! Your personal information is completely private and secure. We never share your birth data with third parties. All calculations are performed using traditional Bazi methods.
Why do I need to enter my exact birth time?
Your birth time is crucial for calculating your Hour Pillar, one of the Four Pillars in Bazi. The hour pillar significantly affects the elemental analysis and timing recommendations. If you don't know your exact birth time, use an approximate time.
Can I use this for other important decisions besides applications?
While Lucky Apply is optimized for application timing (jobs, schools, visas), the Bazi calculations can indicate auspicious periods for any important life decisions where timing matters.
What if I don't get the result I hoped for?
Lucky Apply shows when cosmic energies are most favorable, but success depends on many factors including your preparation, qualifications, and effort. Use the app as a tool to optimize timing, not as a guarantee of outcomes.
