Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Lucky Apply ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, password, and name when you create an account
  • Birth Details: Birth date, birth time, and birth location for Bazi calculations
  • Profile Information: Optional profile photo and preferences

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App features used, reading history, and timestamps
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate your personal Bazi chart and calculate lucky times
  • Provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • Store your reading history
  • Process subscription payments through Apple's In-App Purchase system
  • Send important service notifications
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Respond to your support requests

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Firebase: We use Google Firebase for secure cloud storage and authentication
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Local Storage: Some data is cached locally on your device for offline access
  • Security Measures: We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share limited data with:

  • Apple: For processing In-App Purchases and app distribution
  • Firebase/Google: For hosting and authentication services

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

5. Your Bazi Data

  • Your birth details are used ONLY for Bazi calculations
  • We never share your birth data with third parties
  • All calculations are performed using traditional methods
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time

6. Subscriptions and Payments

  • All payments are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system
  • We do not store your credit card or payment information
  • Subscription information is managed through your Apple ID

7. Children's Privacy

Lucky Apply is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your reading history

To exercise these rights, go to Settings → Privacy & Security in the app, or contact us at support@luckyapply.com.

9. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • Reading history is retained until you delete it or close your account
  • Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@luckyapply.com
  • Website: https://skyliu.github.io/luckyapply
